Born in Beijing and residing in Toronto for most of his life Joe has been tinkering with photography from childhood and continues to explore various aspects of photography. This website intends to present a selection of photos in Joe's collection during his travels and his work in black white analog photography and the darkroom.

Please contact Joe before using his photos or stories for commercial purposes. The website was designed and built while staying in the city of Tashkorgan.
